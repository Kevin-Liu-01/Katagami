Katagami Text is a proportional sans serif for long reading. It is one of the five members of the Katagami family, which are built from one source by the same scripts: Katagami Sans, Katagami Round, Katagami Text, Katagami Serif and Katagami Mono. Katagami Text starts from the outlines of Inter. Its lowercase is lowered so the x-height is 0.71 of the cap height, and every glyph is then set 4 percent smaller with the line height unchanged. Round letters and the narrow capitals are wider, and the heavy weights are lighter: its 700 has the stem weight of 640 in Inter. It has a double-storey a with a straight stem, a compact f, a straight l and a 1 with a long flag.

Katagami Text has a roman and an Italic. Each is a variable font with a weight axis from Thin (100) to Black (900), and each weight in steps of 100 is a named instance. The Italic slants 9.4 degrees. It keeps the letterforms, features and line metrics of the roman.

Katagami draws Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. The roman also sets 28 more scripts, among them Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Georgian, Armenian and Ethiopic. They are merged from other fonts under the SIL Open Font License and scaled to the x-height of Katagami. The Italic sets Latin, Greek and Cyrillic.

Katagami Text is released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
