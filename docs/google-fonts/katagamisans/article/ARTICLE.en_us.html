Katagami Sans is a proportional sans serif for interfaces and display. It is one of the five members of the Katagami family, which are built from one source by the same scripts: Katagami Sans, Katagami Round, Katagami Text, Katagami Serif and Katagami Mono. Katagami Sans starts from the outlines of Inter and moves them toward a squared geometry. Its bowls are superellipses, not circles. The terminals of c, e, s, a and g are cut level, and its dots and punctuation are square. Round letters are condensed, so O, H, o and n have the same width.

Katagami Sans has a roman and an Italic. Each is a variable font with a weight axis from Thin (100) to Black (900), and each weight in steps of 100 is a named instance. The Italic slants 9.4 degrees. It keeps the letterforms, features and line metrics of the roman.

Katagami draws Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. The roman also sets 28 more scripts, among them Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Georgian, Armenian and Ethiopic. They are merged from other fonts under the SIL Open Font License and scaled to the x-height of Katagami. The Italic sets Latin, Greek and Cyrillic.

Katagami Sans is released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
