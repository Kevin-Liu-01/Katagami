Katagami CJK SC is the Simplified Chinese companion to Katagami, a family of five members built from one source by the same scripts: Katagami Sans, Katagami Round, Katagami Text, Katagami Serif and Katagami Mono. Katagami CJK SC is built from Noto Sans SC. It holds the 6,763 hanzi of GB 2312 with its CJK symbols, CJK punctuation and the full-width forms.

The file carries the Latin of Katagami Sans in place of the Latin of Noto Sans SC. It covers the Google Fonts Latin core and the auxiliary letters of its languages, with the kerning, marks, language forms and contextual punctuation of Katagami Sans. Latin in Katagami CJK SC sets as it does in Katagami Sans at every weight.

Katagami CJK SC is a variable font with a weight axis from Thin (100) to Black (900), and each weight in steps of 100 is a named instance. Its line metrics hold the line of the Katagami members, so a page can set it in the same CSS family as a member and fetch it only when the text holds Chinese characters.

Katagami CJK SC is released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
